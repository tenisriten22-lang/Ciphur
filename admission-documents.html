Required Documents

Complete list of documents needed for admission

Academic Documents

10th Standard

  • Original Marks Card
  • Attested Photocopy (2 copies)
  • Pass Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)

Format: Original + 2 photocopies

Attestation: By gazetted officer

12th Standard

  • Original Marks Card
  • Attested Photocopy (2 copies)
  • Pass Certificate
  • Provisional Certificate
  • Character Certificate

Format: Original + 2 photocopies

Attestation: By gazetted officer

Degree Certificate

  • Original Degree Certificate
  • Attested Photocopy (2 copies)
  • Provisional Certificate
  • Consolidated Marks Card
  • Course Completion Certificate

Format: Original + 2 photocopies

For: M.Tech admissions only

Identity Documents

Photo Identity

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID Card
  • Passport
  • Driving License
  • PAN Card (if applicable)

Required: Any one valid ID

Format: Original + photocopy

Photographs

  • Passport Size Photos (6 copies)
  • Stamp Size Photos (4 copies)
  • Recent photos (within 6 months)
  • Light background
  • Clear and visible face

Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm

Format: Printed on photo paper

Birth Certificate

  • Original Birth Certificate
  • Attested Photocopy
  • School Leaving Certificate
  • Affidavit (if not available)

Required: For age verification

Format: Original + photocopy

Entrance Exam Documents

KCET Documents

  • KCET Rank Card
  • KCET Admit Card
  • KCET Score Card
  • Application Acknowledgment
  • Fee Payment Receipt

Format: Original + photocopy

For: KCET candidates

COMEDK Documents

  • COMEDK Rank Card
  • COMEDK Admit Card
  • COMEDK Score Card
  • Application Acknowledgment
  • Fee Payment Receipt

Format: Original + photocopy

For: COMEDK candidates

GATE Documents

  • GATE Score Card
  • GATE Admit Card
  • Application Acknowledgment
  • Fee Payment Receipt
  • Valid ID Proof

Format: Original + photocopy

For: M.Tech candidates

Category Documents (if applicable)

SC/ST Certificate

  • Caste Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • Latest Caste Validity

Issued by: Tahsildar

Validity: Current year

OBC Certificate

  • Caste Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Non-Creamy Layer Certificate
  • Residence Certificate

Issued by: Tahsildar

Income Limit: As per government norms

EWS Certificate

  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • EWS Certificate
  • Affidavit (if required)

Issued by: Tahsildar

Income Limit: As per government norms

Special Category Documents

Sports Quota

  • Sports Participation Certificate
  • Achievement Certificate
  • Level Certificate (District/State/National)
  • Letter from Sports Authority

Level: Minimum district level

Recent: Within 2 years

Physically Disabled

  • Disability Certificate
  • Medical Certificate
  • Government Doctor Certificate
  • Disability Percentage

Minimum: 40% disability

Issued by: Government hospital

NRI/PIO/OCI

  • Passport Copy
  • Visa Copy
  • PIO/OCI Card
  • Sponsor Letter
  • Bank Statement

For: NRI quota candidates

Additional: Embassy documents

Other Important Documents

Transfer Certificate

  • Original TC from previous college
  • Attested photocopy
  • Migration Certificate
  • Conduct Certificate

Required: For all admissions

Format: Original + photocopy

Study Certificate

  • Study Certificate for 7 years
  • Residence Certificate
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Ration Card Copy

For: Karnataka domicile proof

Issued by: School authorities

Income Certificate

  • Family Income Certificate
  • Parent's Income Proof
  • Salary Slips
  • IT Returns

For: Scholarship eligibility

Issued by: Tahsildar

Document Guidelines

Attestation Rules

  • Attestation by gazetted officer only
  • Name and designation of attesting officer
  • Seal and signature required
  • Date of attestation must be recent

Photocopy Guidelines

  • Clear and legible copies
  • Both sides of documents
  • A4 size paper only
  • No self-attestation allowed
