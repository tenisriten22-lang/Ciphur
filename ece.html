Electronics and Communication Engineering

Pioneering innovations in electronics and communication technologies

Department Overview

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at BMS College of Engineering is a hub of innovation and excellence in electronics, communication systems, and emerging technologies.

Our comprehensive program covers VLSI design, embedded systems, digital signal processing, wireless communication, and IoT technologies. Students gain hands-on experience with modern electronic design tools and industry-standard equipment.

With strong research focus and industry collaborations, we prepare students to become leaders in the rapidly evolving field of electronics and communication engineering.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in electronics and communication engineering, fostering innovation and producing engineers who can drive technological advancement in the digital era.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in electronics and communication, promote research and development, nurture innovation, and create technically competent professionals for global challenges.

Laboratories

VLSI Design Lab

Advanced CAD tools, FPGA development boards, and IC design software for VLSI projects.

Communication Systems Lab

Modern communication equipment, spectrum analyzers, and signal processing tools.

Embedded Systems Lab

Microcontroller kits, development boards, and embedded programming environments.

DSP Lab

Digital signal processing kits, MATLAB, and real-time processing systems.

RF & Microwave Lab

Vector network analyzers, signal generators, and antenna measurement systems.

IoT Lab

Sensors, microcontrollers, and IoT platforms for smart device development.
