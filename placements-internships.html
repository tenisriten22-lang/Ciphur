Internship Application Process

Step 1: Registration

Register on the college placement portal and update your resume with academic and project details.

Step 2: Preparation

Attend resume workshops and interview preparation sessions. Work on technical skills relevant to your target companies.

Step 3: Application

Apply for internship opportunities through the portal. Attend company-specific tests and interviews.

Step 4: Selection

Selection based on academic performance, test scores, and interview performance. Receive offer letters.

Step 5: Joining

Complete onboarding formalities and join the internship program with company orientation.

Step 6: Evaluation

Regular performance evaluation and feedback. Complete internship with final presentation and report.
