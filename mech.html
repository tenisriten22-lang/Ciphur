Mechanical Engineering

Engineering excellence through innovation and precision

Department Overview

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at BMS College of Engineering is a cornerstone of engineering education, combining traditional mechanical principles with modern technologies.

Our comprehensive program covers thermal engineering, manufacturing processes, machine design, robotics, and automation. Students gain hands-on experience with modern manufacturing equipment and advanced simulation tools.

With strong industry connections and research focus, we prepare students to become innovative engineers capable of solving complex mechanical challenges in various industries.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in mechanical engineering, producing innovative engineers who can drive technological advancement and contribute to industrial development.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in mechanical engineering, promote research and innovation, develop practical skills, and create competent professionals for global industries.
