Eligibility Criteria

Requirements for admission to various programs

B.E. Programs Eligibility

Academic Requirements

  • 10+2 Education: Must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects
  • Minimum Marks: 45% marks in qualifying examination
  • Reserved Categories: 40% marks for SC/ST/OBC candidates
  • Board Recognition: From recognized board/university

Entrance Exam Requirements

  • KCET: Valid KCET score for Karnataka candidates
  • COMEDK: Valid COMEDK score for other state candidates
  • JEE Main: Valid JEE Main score for all India quota
  • Minimum Score: As specified by respective authorities
  • Rank Criteria: Based on merit and seat availability

Age Requirements

General Category

17-25 years as on 31st December of admission year

SC/ST/OBC

17-27 years as on 31st December of admission year

PWD Candidates

17-30 years as on 31st December of admission year

M.Tech Programs Eligibility

Academic Requirements

  • B.E./B.Tech Degree: Must have passed B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline
  • Minimum Marks: 50% marks in qualifying degree
  • Reserved Categories: 45% marks for SC/ST/OBC candidates
  • University Recognition: From recognized university
  • Discipline Relevance: Must be in relevant engineering discipline

Entrance Exam Requirements

  • GATE: Valid GATE score in relevant discipline
  • PGCET: Valid PGCET score for Karnataka candidates
  • No Age Limit: No upper age limit for M.Tech programs
  • Merit Basis: Both GATE and PGCET candidates considered
  • Seat Allocation: Based on merit and category

Branch-wise Eligibility

Computer Science (CSE)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Information Science (ISE)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Electronics & Comm. (ECE)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Electrical & Electronics (EEE)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Mechanical Engineering (MECH)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Civil Engineering (CIVIL)

  • Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics in 10+2
  • Minimum 45% in PCM
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score

Special Category Eligibility

NRI Candidates

  • Children of NRIs with valid documents
  • Minimum 50% in qualifying examination
  • Separate NRI quota available
  • Direct admission based on merit

Management Quota

  • Management quota seats available
  • Minimum 45% in qualifying examination
  • Based on merit and availability
  • Higher fee structure applicable

Sports Quota

  • National/international level sports persons
  • Valid sports certificates required
  • Minimum 40% in qualifying examination
  • Separate sports quota available

PWD Candidates

  • Physically disabled candidates
  • Valid disability certificate required
  • Minimum 40% in qualifying examination
  • 3% seats reserved

Important Notes

  • All certificates must be original and verified
  • Eligibility criteria may change as per government norms
  • College reserves the right to modify eligibility criteria
  • Candidates must meet all criteria at the time of admission
  • False information will lead to cancellation of admission
  • Disputes subject to Bangalore jurisdiction
