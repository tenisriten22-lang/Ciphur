Campus Life

Vibrant, diverse, and enriching campus experience

Explore Campus Life

BMS College of Engineering offers a vibrant campus life with numerous opportunities for students to explore their interests, develop skills, and create lasting memories.

🎭 Student Clubs

Technical, cultural, and professional clubs for holistic development. IEEE, ACM, Music, Dance, Drama, Literary, Photography, Entrepreneurship, and more.

⚽ Sports & Athletics

Comprehensive sports facilities including indoor and outdoor sports. Basketball, Cricket, Football, Tennis, Volleyball, Athletics, Swimming, and more.

🎨 Cultural Events

Annual festivals, department events, workshops, and competitions. TechnoCultural fest, department festivals, cultural competitions, and workshops.

🏠 Hostel Facilities

Comfortable and secure accommodation with modern amenities. Separate hostels for boys and girls with mess, recreation, and study facilities.

🏛️ Campus Facilities

State-of-the-art infrastructure supporting academic and extracurricular activities. Library, labs, sports complex, cafeteria, and more.
