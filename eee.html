Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Powering the future with sustainable energy solutions

Department Overview

The Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at BMS College of Engineering is dedicated to producing engineers who can meet the growing demands of the power sector and electrical industry.

Our comprehensive program covers power systems, electrical machines, control systems, power electronics, and renewable energy technologies. Students gain expertise in both traditional electrical engineering and modern smart grid technologies.

With state-of-the-art laboratories and strong industry connections, we prepare students to become leaders in the rapidly evolving field of electrical engineering.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in electrical and electronics engineering, producing innovative engineers who can contribute to sustainable energy solutions and technological advancement.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in electrical engineering, promote research in power systems and renewable energy, develop industry-ready professionals, and foster innovation for societal benefit.

Laboratories

Power Systems Lab

Advanced power system simulation software and hardware for transmission and distribution studies.

Electrical Machines Lab

Various AC and DC machines, transformers, and testing equipment for machine analysis.

Power Electronics Lab

Power electronic converters, drives, and modern power electronic devices.

Control Systems Lab

Control system simulation tools, PLCs, and industrial automation equipment.

Renewable Energy Lab

Solar panels, wind turbine models, and energy storage systems.

High Voltage Lab

High voltage testing equipment and insulation testing facilities.
