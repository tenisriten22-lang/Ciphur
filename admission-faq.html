Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries about admissions at BMSCE

General Admission Questions

What is the admission procedure for B.E. programs?

Admissions are based on KCET/COMEDK scores for government quota and merit for management quota. Candidates must appear for counseling, document verification, and seat allocation based on merit and preferences.

Are there scholarships available for students?

Yes, scholarships are available for meritorious students, economically weaker sections, and reserved categories as per government norms. Institutional scholarships are also available for deserving students.

Is hostel accommodation available for all students?

Yes, separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls with modern amenities and 24/7 security. Hostel admission is subject to availability and separate application process.

What is the placement record of BMSCE?

BMS has an excellent placement record with 95% placement rate. Top companies like Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Infosys, TCS, and many others recruit from campus regularly.

Are there any entrance exams for admission?

Yes, KCET for Karnataka students, COMEDK for other states, and JEE Main for all India quota. Management quota admissions are based on merit without entrance exams.

Eligibility Questions

What are the eligibility criteria for B.E. programs?

10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved categories). Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score required. Age limit: 17-25 years as on 31st December.

Can I apply if I have compartment in 12th?

No, candidates must have passed all subjects in 12th standard without compartment at the time of admission. Compartment results must be cleared before admission.

Is there any age limit for admission?

Yes, 17-25 years for general category, 17-27 years for SC/ST/OBC, and 17-30 years for PWD candidates as on 31st December of admission year.

What subjects are required in 12th for engineering?

Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are compulsory subjects in 12th standard for engineering admissions. Candidates must have passed all three subjects.

Are diploma holders eligible for direct admission?

Yes, diploma holders can apply for lateral entry admission to 2nd year B.E. programs based on merit and availability of seats.

Application Process Questions

How do I apply for admission?

Register online through KCET/COMEDK websites or college website for management quota. Fill application form, upload documents, pay fees, attend counseling, and complete admission formalities.

What documents are required for admission?

10th and 12th marks cards, transfer certificate, migration certificate, rank card, photos, ID proof, category certificates (if applicable), and other relevant documents.

What is the application fee?

Application fee varies by program: KCET/COMEDK - ₹500-1000, Management quota - ₹1000. Application fee is non-refundable.

When does the admission process start?

Admission process usually starts in March with KCET/COMEDK applications. Management quota applications start in May. Regular classes begin in August.

How can I track my application status?

Application status can be tracked through the respective admission portals using your application number and date of birth.

Fee Structure Questions

What is the fee structure for B.E. programs?

Government quota: ₹60,000-80,000 per year. Management quota: ₹1,50,000-2,00,000 per year. Additional fees for hostel, transportation, and other facilities.

Are there any additional fees besides tuition fee?

Yes, additional fees include university fee, library fee, laboratory fee, development fee, examination fee, and other miscellaneous charges.

How much is the hostel fee?

Hostel fees range from ₹1,15,000 to ₹1,67,000 per year including room rent, mess fees, and other facilities. Varies by room type and occupancy.

Can fees be paid in installments?

Yes, fees can be paid semester-wise. Hostel fees can be paid monthly with approval. Special installment options available for deserving cases.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept online payments (net banking, credit/debit cards, UPI), demand drafts, and bank challans. Cash payment is limited.

Campus Life Questions

What are the hostel facilities like?

Hostels have spacious rooms, modern furniture, attached bathrooms, Wi-Fi, mess facilities, recreation rooms, gymnasium, 24/7 security, and medical facilities.

Are there sports facilities available?

Yes, comprehensive sports facilities including indoor games (basketball, badminton, table tennis) and outdoor sports (football, cricket, tennis, volleyball, athletics track, swimming pool).

What student clubs are available?

25+ student clubs including technical (IEEE, ACM, Robotics), cultural (Music, Dance, Drama), professional (Entrepreneurship, Business), and recreational clubs.

Is transportation facility available?

Yes, fleet of 25+ buses covering major routes in Bangalore. Transportation fee is ₹20,000 per year. Routes include Electronic City, Whitefield, Jayanagar, etc.

What are the library facilities?

Central library with 100,000+ books, digital resources, study halls, online databases, 24/7 access, and reprographic services.

Placement Questions

What is the placement percentage?

BMS has 95% placement rate with 220+ recruiting companies. Average package is 10 LPA and highest package is 43 LPA.

Which companies recruit from campus?

Top companies include Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Apple, Meta, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Accenture, Deloitte, Bosch, Intel, and many more.

What training is provided for placements?

Comprehensive training including technical skills, aptitude development, soft skills, interview preparation, mock interviews, and industry mentorship.

Are internship opportunities available?

Yes, 85% of students complete internships with 78% conversion to pre-placement offers. Summer internships, year-long projects, and research internships available.

What is the average salary package?

Average package is 10 LPA. Branch-wise: CSE 12 LPA, ISE 11 LPA, ECE 9 LPA, EEE 8 LPA, MECH 8.5 LPA, CIVIL 7 LPA.
