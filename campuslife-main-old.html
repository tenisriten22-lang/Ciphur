Campus Life

Vibrant, diverse, and enriching campus experience

Student Clubs

Technical Clubs

IEEE Student Chapter, ACM Club, Robotics Club, Coding Club, IoT Club, and various technical forums for innovation and skill development.

Cultural Clubs

Music Club, Dance Club, Drama Club, Literary Club, Photography Club, and Art Club for creative expression and cultural activities.

Professional Clubs

Entrepreneurship Cell, Business Club, Debate Club, Quiz Club, and Leadership Development programs for professional growth.

Sports & Athletics

Indoor Sports

  • Basketball courts with modern facilities
  • Badminton courts with professional lighting
  • Table tennis tables and equipment
  • Chess and carrom rooms
  • Fully equipped gymnasium

Outdoor Sports

  • Football ground with natural turf
  • Cricket ground with pitch and pavilion
  • Tennis courts with professional surface
  • Volleyball courts
  • Athletics track and field facilities

Sports Achievements

Our teams regularly participate and excel in university-level tournaments, inter-college competitions, and state championships. We have won multiple trophies in cricket, basketball, football, and athletics.

Cultural Events

Annual Fest - TechnoCultural

Three-day extravaganza combining technical competitions, cultural performances, workshops, and celebrity performances.

Department Festivals

Each department organizes annual festivals showcasing technical innovations, cultural programs, and departmental achievements.

Workshops & Seminars

Regular workshops on emerging technologies, cultural activities, personality development, and skill enhancement programs.

Campus Facilities

Library

Central library with over 100,000 books, digital resources, journals, and study spaces with modern amenities.

Cafeteria

Multi-cuisine cafeteria serving hygienic and nutritious food with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.

Health Center

Medical facility with qualified doctors, nurses, and emergency care available 24/7 for student health needs.

Transportation

Fleet of buses covering major routes in Bengaluru for safe and convenient transportation for day scholars.

Banking

On-campus bank branch and ATM facilities for convenient banking services and financial transactions.

Wi-Fi Campus

High-speed internet connectivity throughout the campus including classrooms, labs, library, and common areas.
