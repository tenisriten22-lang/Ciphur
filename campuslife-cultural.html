Cultural Events

Celebrating creativity, talent, and diversity

Annual Techno-Cultural Fest - TechnoCultural

About TechnoCultural

Our flagship three-day extravaganza combining technical competitions, cultural performances, workshops, and celebrity performances. It's the biggest college festival in Bangalore, attracting participants from over 50 colleges.

When: March 15-17, 2026

Theme: Innovation Meets Tradition

Expected Participation: 10,000+ students

Events Categories

  • Technical Competitions (Coding, Robotics, Hackathons)
  • Cultural Performances (Music, Dance, Drama)
  • Workshops and Seminars
  • Art and Photography Exhibitions
  • Gaming and eSports Tournaments
  • Celebrity Night Performances

Department Festivals

CSE Fest - CodeFest

Annual computer science festival focusing on coding competitions, hackathons, and tech talks.

Highlights: 48-hour hackathon, AI workshops, coding contests

When: February every year

ECE Fest - ElectroFest

Electronics and communication festival with circuit design, robotics, and innovation projects.

Highlights: Circuit competitions, robotics showcase, IoT workshops

When: November every year

MECH Fest - MechFest

Mechanical engineering festival featuring design competitions, CAD workshops, and automotive events.

Highlights: Design contests, CAD workshops, Go-kart competition

When: January every year

CIVIL Fest - CivilFest

Civil engineering festival with bridge building, surveying competitions, and sustainability projects.

Highlights: Bridge building, surveying contests, green building projects

When: October every year

ISE Fest - InfoFest

Information science festival focusing on data analytics, business intelligence, and enterprise systems.

Highlights: Data analytics contests, BI workshops, case studies

When: December every year

EEE Fest - ElectroFest

Electrical and electronics festival with power systems, renewable energy, and automation projects.

Highlights: Power system projects, renewable energy demos, automation contests

When: September every year

Workshops & Seminars

Technical Workshops

Topics: AI/ML, Cloud Computing, Blockchain, IoT, Cybersecurity

Frequency: Monthly

Cultural Workshops

Personality Development

Topics: Public speaking, leadership, time management, stress management

Frequency: Weekly

Industry Seminars

Guest lectures and seminars by industry experts and thought leaders.

Speakers: Industry leaders, entrepreneurs, tech experts

Frequency: Bi-monthly

Art & Craft Workshops

Entrepreneurship Workshops

Frequency: Quarterly
