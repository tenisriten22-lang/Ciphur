Department Overview

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BMS College of Engineering is a premier destination for aspiring technologists and innovators. Established with a vision to excel in computer education and research, our department has consistently been at the forefront of technological advancement.

Our comprehensive curriculum blends theoretical foundations with practical applications, ensuring students develop strong problem-solving skills and technical expertise. We offer specialized tracks in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.

With state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty members, and strong industry connections, we provide an ecosystem that nurtures innovation and prepares students for successful careers in the rapidly evolving tech landscape.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in computer science education and research, producing innovative technologists who drive digital transformation and contribute to societal advancement.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through cutting-edge curriculum, foster research and innovation, develop industry-ready professionals, and instill ethical values for sustainable technological development.
