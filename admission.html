Admission Channels

KCET Quota

For Karnataka domicile students through KCET counseling.

Seats: 45% of total intake

Based on: KCET rank and merit

COMEDK Quota

For non-Karnataka students through COMEDK counseling.

Seats: 30% of total intake

Based on: COMEDK rank and merit

Management Quota

Direct admission based on merit and availability.

Seats: 25% of total intake

Based on: Academic merit
