Application Process

Step-by-step guide to secure your admission

Application Process Overview

Step 1: Registration

Register online through the official college website or respective entrance exam portals. Fill in personal and academic details accurately.

Required: Basic personal information, educational details

Time: 15-20 minutes

Step 2: Document Upload

Upload required documents including marks cards, certificates, and identity proof. Ensure all documents are clear and legible.

Documents: Marks cards, certificates, photos

Format: PDF/JPEG, maximum size 2MB

Step 3: Fee Payment

Pay the application fee through online payment gateway. Keep the payment receipt for future reference.

Amount: ₹500-1000 (varies by program)

Methods: Net banking, credit/debit card, UPI

Step 4: Entrance Exam

Appear for KCET/COMEDK/JEE Main as applicable. Download and print your admit card and score card.

Preparation: Study previous year papers

Results: Available online on official websites

Step 5: Counseling

Attend counseling sessions as per schedule. Document verification and seat allocation based on merit and preferences.

Documents: Original documents for verification

Process: Choice filling, seat allotment

Step 6: Admission Confirmation

Confirm admission by paying the required fees and completing all formalities within the specified time.

Fees: Tuition fees, hostel fees (if applicable)

Deadline: Usually 3-5 days after seat allotment

KCET Admission Process

Eligibility

  • Karnataka domicile candidates
  • Valid KCET score
  • Meet eligibility criteria
  • Document verification completed

Process Steps

  • Option entry (choice filling)
  • Seat allotment rounds
  • College reporting

Management Quota Process

Eligibility

  • Meet basic eligibility criteria
  • Minimum 45% in qualifying exam
  • Valid documents
  • Available seats

Process Steps

  • Direct application to college
  • Document verification
  • Personal interview (if required)
  • Seat allotment based on merit

Management Quota Features

  • Direct admission without entrance exam
  • Higher fee structure
  • Limited seats available
  • Based on merit and availability

Required Documents

Academic Documents

  • 10th Standard Marks Card
  • 12th Standard Marks Card
  • Degree Certificate (for PG)
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate

Identity Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Voter ID/Passport
  • Passport Size Photos (6)
  • Birth Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)

Entrance Exam Documents

  • KCET/COMEDK Rank Card
  • GATE Score Card (for M.Tech)
  • Admit Cards
  • Score Cards
  • Application Acknowledgment

Application Tips

Before Applying

  • Prepare all required documents
  • Research programs and colleges
  • Understand fee structure
  • Check important dates

During Application

  • Fill form accurately
  • Upload clear documents
  • Pay fees on time
  • Save application number
