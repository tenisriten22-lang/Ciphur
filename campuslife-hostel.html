Room Types

Single Occupancy

Private rooms for individual students with attached bathroom.

Features: Private bathroom, study area, wardrobe, internet

Capacity: 1 student per room

Availability: Limited, first-come basis

Double Occupancy

Shared rooms for two students with common bathroom.

Features: Shared bathroom, individual study areas, storage

Capacity: 2 students per room

Availability: Good availability

Triple Occupancy

Economical option for three students with shared facilities.

Features: Shared bathroom, study areas, storage

Capacity: 3 students per room

Availability: Most economical option

Hostel Rules & Regulations

General Rules

  • Strict adherence to timing schedules
  • No visitors allowed without permission
  • Maintenance of discipline and decorum
  • Regular attendance in college
  • Proper use of hostel facilities

Safety Guidelines

  • No electrical appliances in rooms
  • No smoking or alcohol consumption
  • Fire safety protocols to be followed
  • Emergency contact numbers displayed
  • Regular safety drills conducted
