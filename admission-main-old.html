Admissions

Your gateway to excellence in engineering education

Eligibility Criteria

B.E. Programs

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Minimum 45% marks in qualifying examination
  • Valid KCET/COMEDK/JEE score
  • Age limit: 17-25 years as on 31st December

M.Tech Programs

  • B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline
  • Minimum 50% marks in qualifying degree
  • Valid GATE/PGCET score
  • No age limit for postgraduate programs

Reserved Categories

Government norms apply for SC/ST/OBC and other reserved categories. Relaxation in eligibility criteria as per Karnataka Government rules.

How to Apply

Step 1: Registration

Register online through the official college website or KCET/COMEDK portal. Fill in personal and academic details accurately.

Step 2: Document Submission

Submit required documents including marks cards, certificates, and identity proof. Upload scanned copies as per specifications.

Step 3: Counseling

Attend counseling sessions as per schedule. Document verification and seat allocation based on merit and preferences.

Application Process

Applications are accepted through both merit and management quotas. Merit seats are filled through KCET/COMEDK counseling, while management seats are filled through direct admission based on merit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the admission procedure for B.E. programs?

Admissions are based on KCET/COMEDK scores for government quota and merit for management quota. Candidates must appear for counseling and document verification.

Are there scholarships available?

Yes, scholarships are available for meritorious students, economically weaker sections, and reserved categories as per government norms.

Is hostel accommodation available?

Yes, separate hostel facilities are available for boys and girls with modern amenities and 24/7 security.

What is the placement record?

BMS has an excellent placement record with 95% placement rate and top companies recruiting from campus.

Are there any entrance exams for admission?

Yes, KCET for Karnataka students, COMEDK for other states, and JEE Main for all India quota.
